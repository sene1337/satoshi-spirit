# Bitcoin Maximalism Quiz — Phase 1

Probing questions to surface real convictions. Not trivia — these reveal whether someone is a casual holder or a true maximalist. Adapt and follow up based on responses. You don't need to ask all of them — read the room.

---

## Monetary Theory / Sound Money

1. **Why is Bitcoin money?** What properties does it have that fiat doesn't — and which matter most to you?

2. **"Deflation causes economic stagnation."** Agree or disagree? Why do mainstream economists get this wrong (or right)?

3. **Where does Bitcoin's value come from?** Is it the network, the scarcity, the energy, the game theory — or something else?

## Self-Custody & Sovereignty

4. **Should every Bitcoiner run their own node?** Where's the line between ideal and practical?

5. **"Not your keys, not your coins."** How literally do you take this? Do you think most people will ever self-custody?

6. **What's your custody setup?** Multisig, hardware wallet, geographic distribution — how far do you go?

## Privacy

7. **Is on-chain privacy a right or a feature?** Should Bitcoin be fully private by default?

8. **CoinJoin, PayJoin, silent payments — where do you stand?** Are privacy tools essential infrastructure or nice-to-haves?

9. **Would you use a Bitcoin service that requires KYC?** Under what circumstances, if any?

## Mining & Energy

10. **"Bitcoin wastes energy."** How do you respond to this — and do you think the energy argument matters for adoption?

11. **Should Bitcoin mining be geographically distributed at all costs?** What trade-offs are acceptable?

12. **Proof of work vs. proof of stake — is this a settled debate or is there room for nuance?**

## Altcoins & Shitcoins

13. **Are all altcoins scams, or are some legitimate?** Where exactly do you draw the line?

14. **Ethereum — technology worth studying, competitor, or straight-up scam?** Be specific about why.

15. **"Blockchain technology" without Bitcoin — does it have any value?**

## Regulation, KYC/AML & Taxation

16. **Should Bitcoiners comply with KYC/AML laws?** Is compliance pragmatic or a betrayal of principles?

17. **Is taxation theft?** How does this belief (or rejection of it) shape how you interact with Bitcoin?

18. **If a government banned Bitcoin, what would you do?** Not hypothetically — what would you *actually* do?

## Lightning & Scaling

19. **Is Lightning the scaling solution, or a stopgap?** What concerns you about it, if anything?

20. **Should Bitcoin ever increase the block size?** Under what conditions, if any?

## Bitcoin's Role in Society & Geopolitics

21. **Does Bitcoin fix the world, or just fix money?** How far does "Bitcoin fixes this" extend for you?

22. **Nation-state Bitcoin adoption (El Salvador, etc.) — bullish or dangerous?** Why?

23. **What's the endgame?** Hyperbitcoinization, parallel economy, store of value only — what does success look like?

## Maximalism vs Pluralism

24. **Are you a Bitcoin maximalist?** Define what that means to you — and what it doesn't mean.

25. **What's the strongest argument against Bitcoin that you can't fully refute?** Be honest.

---

## How to Use These

- Start with 2-3 broad questions (1, 24, 23) to calibrate intensity
- Follow threads — if they light up on privacy, dig deeper there
- Push past surface answers: "Why?" and "What convinced you?" and "What would change your mind?"
- Listen for contradictions — those are the most interesting places to explore
- Aim for 5-10 strong positions before moving to Phase 2
