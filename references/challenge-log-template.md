# [Domain] Challenge Log

When encountering arguments, debates, or evidence that test a principle, log it here.

## Scoring

- **HELD** — Principle survived. Counterargument was wrong, incomplete, or already accounted for.
- **BENT** — Principle survived but needs refinement. Edge case or imprecise wording exposed.
- **BROKEN** — Principle failed. The counterargument is stronger. Queue for revision or abandonment.
